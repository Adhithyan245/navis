Navis groups relocation work into four phases. Each checklist item declares
which phase it belongs to via `frontmatter.stage`; the frontend groups items
by stage to render a timeline.

## pre_arrival

Things that must happen before you physically move. Paperwork that needs the
old address, advance bookings, document collection, notice periods at the
current home, preliminary research on the destination neighborhood.

## week_1

The first seven days at the new place. Time-sensitive registrations,
essentials that block daily life (electricity, water, internet, getting to
work), first-pass orientation in the immediate area around your home base.

## month_1

The first 30 days. Identity / address updates, banking and tax setup,
choosing a primary care doctor, mail forwarding from the old address,
exploring beyond your immediate neighborhood.

## month_3

Three-month horizon. Anything that has a 30-90 day deadline (driving
licence transfers in many places, school enrollment for the next term,
community-building, longer-term lease decisions).

Items not pinned to a phase use `stage: universal` and apply at any time.
