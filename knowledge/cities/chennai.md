Chennai is dense and walkable in pockets but car-dependent overall. The Metro
(Blue + Green lines) covers the central spine from Airport through T Nagar,
Anna Nagar, and into Washermanpet — most exploration questions should anchor
on a metro station rather than a free-form address.

High-signal neighborhoods for `explore_city` queries:

  * **T Nagar / Pondy Bazaar** — high-density retail and South Indian food. Loud
    afternoons, calmer evenings after 9 PM. Best for first-time visitors who
    want a single anchor.
  * **Besant Nagar / Elliot's Beach** — coastal, evening-oriented. Cafes, live
    music, beachfront walks. Weak transit; rideshare needed.
  * **Mylapore** — temple district. Kapaleeshwarar temple is the spatial anchor;
    morning markets are the reason to come early.
  * **Anna Nagar / Nungambakkam** — work-friendly cafes, coworking spaces, and
    the densest cluster of long-stay-friendly amenities.

Climate gates: April through June is unforgivably hot — outdoor "explore"
recommendations during those months should default to indoor venues or
post-sunset slots. The northeast monsoon (Oct-Dec) makes beach and outdoor
event recommendations unreliable; bias toward indoor venues with covered access.

Food defaults: filter coffee, idli/dosa, and Chettinad are the genre-defining
options. A user query for "cafes" almost always means modern third-wave coffee
rather than traditional filter coffee — surface both but lead with the modern
interpretation unless context says otherwise.
