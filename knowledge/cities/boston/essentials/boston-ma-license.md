Massachusetts requires new residents to transfer their out-of-state or
foreign driver's licence within 30 days. The RMV (Registry of Motor
Vehicles) handles this; appointments are mandatory and book out weeks in
advance, so reserve your slot the same day you arrive.

Bring: proof of identity (passport / ID card), proof of MA residency (a
signed lease or utility bill), Social Security card or denial letter, and
your existing licence. International transfers usually need a written and
sometimes road test; same-country transfers (from another US state) are
typically a paperwork-only swap.

If you don't plan to drive, a MA State ID from the RMV serves the same
identity-document purpose.
