The MBTA is Boston's metro / subway / bus network. The CharlieCard
(reloadable plastic card) is the cheapest way to pay; a paper CharlieTicket
works too but costs more per ride.

If you'll use transit daily for work, a monthly LinkPass (subway + local
bus) is cheaper than per-ride fares after about 30 trips. Some Boston-area
employers subsidize transit passes via Perq or similar — check before
buying directly.

Pick up a card at any subway station fare-vending machine, or order online
for home delivery.
