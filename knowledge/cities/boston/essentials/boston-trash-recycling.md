Boston's trash and recycling pickup runs on a weekly schedule that varies
by street. Look up your street on the city's "Trash Day" finder (link
above) and add the day to your calendar.

Curb-out rules: bags or city-issued bins out after 5pm the night before,
recycling in the blue carts only (or clear plastic bags in older streets),
no loose trash. Yard waste runs seasonally on a different cycle. Fines
for early/late or wrong-bin set-out are routine — the city does enforce.

Apartment buildings often handle this for you via a dumpster; check with
your building manager before relying on the city schedule.
