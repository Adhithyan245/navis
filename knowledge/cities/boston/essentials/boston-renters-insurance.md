Many Boston landlords now require renters insurance as a condition of the
lease — proof of policy is usually due before move-in. Even when not
required, it is cheap (typically $10-20/month for $20-30k contents
coverage plus liability) and covers theft, water damage from upstairs
neighbours, and temporary accommodation if your unit becomes
uninhabitable.

Common providers in Boston: Lemonade, State Farm, GEICO, Allstate, USAA
(military households). Compare a few — quotes vary by 2-3x for the same
coverage.

Set the policy start date to your lease start date, not your physical
arrival date.
