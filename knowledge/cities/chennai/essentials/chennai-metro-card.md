The Chennai Metro Blue + Green lines cover the central spine from the
airport through T Nagar, Anna Nagar, and into Washermanpet. A rechargeable
travel card (Chennai Metro Card or the National Common Mobility Card) is
faster than buying paper tickets every trip and gives a small discount.

Buy at any metro station ticket counter — bring an ID and small cash for
the initial top-up. The card also works on some MTC buses.

If you'll commute daily, look up the monthly trip-card options at the
station; cost-per-trip drops noticeably above ~50 trips/month.
