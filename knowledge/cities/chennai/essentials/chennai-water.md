Most Chennai apartments use a mix of Metrowater (municipal piped supply)
and private water tankers for non-drinking use. If you're renting, the
landlord typically owns the Metrowater connection; confirm with them how
the bill is settled (often included in rent or maintenance).

For drinking water, almost everyone in Chennai buys 20-litre cans from a
local supplier — your building's caretaker can usually point you to one.
Set up a recurring delivery within the first week.

In the dry months (Mar-Jun) Metrowater supply is intermittent; check if
your building has an overhead tank and a sump, and whether the lift pump
runs on its schedule.
