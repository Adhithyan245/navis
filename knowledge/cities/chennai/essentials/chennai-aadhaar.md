Aadhaar is the address-of-record for almost every other Indian service —
bank KYC, mobile SIM activation, gas connection, electricity, school
admission. Update it before any of those go wrong.

You can update online via the myAadhaar portal (digital proof of address
needed: utility bill, lease, or passport with new address) for a small
fee, or in person at a UIDAI Aadhaar Seva Kendra. Online turnaround is
typically 5-10 days; in-person updates can be same-day.

Bring: original Aadhaar card / number, one proof of address document
issued within the last 3 months. SMS confirmation comes when the update
is approved — keep it; some services ask for the update reference number.
