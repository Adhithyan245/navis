Tamil Nadu Electricity Board (TNEB / TANGEDCO) is the sole electricity
provider in Chennai. For rentals, you typically don't need a new
connection — the meter is already in the landlord's name; just make sure
the meter is read at move-in and the next bill is sent to you (or
included in your rent, per the lease).

If you're buying or moving into a previously unoccupied unit, apply for
a name change ("Name Transfer") at the local TNEB section office or
online. You'll need: existing latest bill, sale-deed or registered lease,
ID proof, and a small fee.

Bills come bi-monthly. Auto-pay via UPI mandate or net banking is the
norm; late payments incur a small surcharge and eventually disconnection.
