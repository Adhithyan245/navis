Most postal services offer a paid mail-forwarding option (USPS in the US,
Royal Mail in the UK, India Post's address update service in India). Start
it a week before you move so anything in transit on moving day still
reaches you.

A short forwarding window (3-6 months) is usually enough — by then most
senders should have your new address via the address-change step. Keep
careful notes of anything that still arrives via forwarding so you can
chase the sender to update directly.
