Health insurance is the priority — gaps in coverage compound badly if
something goes wrong. If your existing plan does not cover the new
location, get short-term cover until your new long-term plan starts.

Then renters / contents insurance for the new place, and auto insurance
if you're keeping a car (most policies are region-locked and will not pay
out at a new address you didn't declare). Life insurance is usually
portable but ask your insurer to confirm.
