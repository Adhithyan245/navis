Pick a primary care doctor (GP / PCP / family physician) within 30 days
of arriving. Waiting until you're sick is worse — most clinics have a
multi-week registration backlog, and being a new patient means more
paperwork than a routine visit.

If you have a family, register children with a pediatrician and any adults
with their own GP at the same time. Some countries (UK, Spain, much of
continental Europe) require you to register with a specific local
practice tied to your address; others (US, India) let you pick freely.
Check what's true for your destination before choosing.
