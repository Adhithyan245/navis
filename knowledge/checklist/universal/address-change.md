Update your address with the services that send you statements or
deliveries: banks and credit cards, employer payroll, subscription services
(streaming, magazines), online retailers you order from regularly, and any
government bodies that hold a residential address on file.

Order roughly: bank → employer → government → subscriptions. Late address
updates can trigger fraud alerts on cards used at the new location, so
banks first.
