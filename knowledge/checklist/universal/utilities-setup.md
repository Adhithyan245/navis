Aim to have electricity, water (where it's separately billed), and
internet active on your first day or the day after. Most providers can
schedule activation a week or two in advance — call before the move.

Internet is often the slowest to set up (engineer visits, hardware
delivery). If yours will not be live for several days, ask your provider
for a temporary mobile hotspot or use a local SIM with generous data
while you wait.
