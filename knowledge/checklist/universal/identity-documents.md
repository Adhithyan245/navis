Identity documents — driver's licence, passport, national ID — usually
have address-update deadlines defined by local law (often 7-30 days).
Missing the window can mean fines or invalidated documents.

Order to do them: whatever is fastest first (often a website update for
ID/national registration), then driver's licence (often needs an in-person
visit), then passport (slowest, but a passport mismatch with your other
documents causes problems at banks and airports). Bring proof of new
address: a utility bill or signed lease usually works.
