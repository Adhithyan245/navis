Update the residential address on every account, debit/credit card, and
loan. If you've moved between countries, also consider opening a local
checking account — many local services (utilities, gym memberships, even
some employers) charge less or refuse foreign cards.

If you keep a foreign account active, set its address to your new one
anyway: card fraud detection uses billing address as a signal, and a
mismatched address triggers blocks at random.
