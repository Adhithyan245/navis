Save these in your phone in the first week: local emergency number (911,
112, 999, 100, etc.), nearest hospital with an ER, a 24-hour pharmacy in
your area, a non-emergency police line, a trusted neighbor or building
manager, and your country's embassy or consulate if you've moved
internationally.

If you have children or anyone with a medical condition at home, also note
their nearest specialist clinic and any prescriptions that need re-filling
locally. Print a small paper card with the same info — phones die.
